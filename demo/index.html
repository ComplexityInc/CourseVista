How a course gets filmed without anyone turning up

  1. 01 You send what you have Photographs, a hole map, a scorecard diagram, an old brochure shoot. Whatever exists. Phone photos are fine.
  2. 02 Each hole is built tee to green One continuous film per hole, matched to how the hole actually plays — the dogleg where the dogleg is, the bunkers where the bunkers are.
  3. 03 Nothing on the course changes No tee sheet cleared, no closure, no drone overhead, no members inconvenienced, no committee approval for site access.
